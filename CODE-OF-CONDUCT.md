# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We, the contributors to Suraksha Loop, commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

This project exists to protect workers who are often unheard. **We hold ourselves to the same respect we ask the product to show them.**

## Our standards
Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, including across languages and regions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the people the project serves and for the community as a whole
- Being patient under hackathon pressure: sleep-deprived teammates deserve extra kindness

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as a phone number, address or recordings) without explicit permission
- Mocking anyone's accent, dialect, language or literacy
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Project-specific commitments
Because Suraksha Loop handles the voices and safety of real workers:
1. **Consent first.** We never record, store or share anyone's voice, image or words without informed consent.
2. **No blame, no surveillance.** We never build features that score, rank or discipline individual workers, and we never add face recognition or biometric identification.
3. **Honesty.** We report our metrics truthfully, label synthetic data as synthetic, and never overstate what the AI can do. That applies to judges, users and each other.
4. **Respect for the rules of the event.** We disclose pre-event preparation and attribute all third-party work.

## Enforcement responsibilities
The team captain and the repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, chats and calls, the INDUX 5.0-2026 venue and events, and wherever an individual officially represents the project.

At the hackathon venue, the organisers' own code of conduct and rules also apply. Where they differ, the stricter standard applies.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers (the repository owner, via GitHub). For incidents at the INDUX venue, you may also contact the organisers at indux5.0_hackathon@vikrantuniversity.ac.in. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines
1. **Correction:** for inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour. A private, written warning with clarity about what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** for a single serious incident or a series of actions. A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban:** for a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour. A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people. A permanent ban from the project.
